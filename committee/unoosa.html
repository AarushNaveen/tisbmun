About the committee

The United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs promotes international cooperation in the peaceful use and exploration of outer space. Based in Vienna, it serves as the secretariat of the Committee on the Peaceful Uses of Outer Space and helps countries apply the international treaties that govern activity in space.

Its work spans space law and policy, the registration of objects launched into orbit, the sustainability of space activities and the use of space-based data for development and disaster response. In committee, delegates work through questions of access, safety and responsibility in a domain shared by every nation.
