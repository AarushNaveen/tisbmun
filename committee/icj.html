About the committee

The International Court of Justice is the principal judicial organ of the United Nations. Created by the UN Charter and based at the Peace Palace in The Hague, it decides legal disputes that states bring before it and, when asked by UN bodies entitled to do so, gives advisory opinions on points of international law.

The Court is composed of 15 judges elected by the General Assembly and the Security Council for nine-year terms. In Model UN simulations of the Court, participants typically serve as advocates presenting each side of a case or as judges who question the evidence and deliver a reasoned judgment grounded in international law.
